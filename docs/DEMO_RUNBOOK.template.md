## Five-Minute Presentation

1. Enter question and coin.
2. Show Research Plan.
3. Show Collector/Execution status.
4. Show market judgment.
5. Open one Evidence item.
6. Show supporting and contradicting Evidence.
7. Explain confidence limiters.
8. Show Execution Log and artifacts.

## Backup Switch

Live failure procedure:

1. Stop waiting after the agreed threshold.
2. Open `demo-fixtures/competition-ready/live-success/` or offline backup.
3. Show the failure/fallback entry in Execution Log.
4. Continue with the saved report and Evidence.
